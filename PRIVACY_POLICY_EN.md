# Stocky Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 5, 2026

Stocky ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Stocky inventory management application, how it is used, and how it is protected.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
You need to create an account to use the application. The following information is collected during this process:
- **Email address:** For account creation, authentication, and password reset
- **Password:** Stored securely in hashed form (bcrypt); never stored as plain text
- **Google account information (optional):** If you sign in with Google, the email address and authentication token provided by Google are used. We do not have access to your Google account password.

### 1.2 Business Data
All business data you enter within the app is stored on our servers:
- **Product information:** Product name, barcode, stock quantity, purchase/sale prices, tax rates, unit, storage location, product descriptions
- **Customer information:** Name, phone, email, address, balance, notes
- **Supplier information:** Company name, contact details, tax number, IBAN, bank details, address, notes
- **Transaction records:** Sales, purchases, returns, collections, and payment transactions; product items, discounts, payment method, and date information

### 1.3 Company Information
Information stored for display on receipts/invoices:
- Company name, address, phone, email, website
- Tax number, tax office
- Company logo (uploaded to Supabase Storage)

### 1.4 Subscription and Payment Information
- **Subscription status:** Plan type (free/monthly/yearly), expiration date, receipt print counter
- **Purchase token:** A token generated by Google Play Billing, used for subscription verification
- Your credit card or payment information is **not collected or stored** by us; all payment transactions are processed through the Google Play infrastructure

### 1.5 Receipt Settings
- Paper width, auto-print preference
- Information display preferences for receipts (company name, logo, address, phone, email, website, tax info)
- Custom header and footer texts
- Stocky branding visibility preference (premium feature)

### 1.6 Data Stored on Device
Some preferences are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences) and are not sent to our servers:
- Theme preference (light/dark/system)
- Language preference
- Barcode scanner sound and vibration settings
- Bluetooth printer connection details (MAC address, device name)
- User settings, company info, and receipt settings cache (for fast access)

### 1.7 User Settings
The following business preferences are stored on our servers:
- Default tax rate
- Prices include tax format
- Currency
- Overselling permission setting

## 2. Device Permissions and Their Purposes

### 2.1 Camera Access
Camera access is requested for the barcode scanning feature:
- No photos or videos are recorded
- Camera footage is not sent to our servers
- Barcode scanning is performed entirely on-device (Google ML Kit)

### 2.2 Contacts Access (Optional)
When adding customers or suppliers, you can select contacts from your phone's contact list:
- This feature is completely optional
- Your contact data is not sent to our servers
- Only the selected contact's information (name, phone, email, address) is transferred to the relevant form

### 2.3 Bluetooth Access
Bluetooth permissions are requested for connecting to thermal printers:
- Used solely for communicating with the printer device
- Device information collected via Bluetooth (printer name, MAC address) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers

### 2.4 Internet and Network State
- Used for data synchronization, authentication, and subscription verification

### 2.5 Vibration
- Used for barcode scanning feedback

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
|------------|---------|-----------|
| **Internet** | Data synchronization and authentication | Yes |
| **Network State** | Offline/online status check | Yes |
| **Camera** | Barcode scanning | No |
| **Contacts** | Transfer contact info to forms | No |
| **Bluetooth** | Thermal printer connection | No |
| **Vibration** | Barcode scanning feedback | No |

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Service delivery:** Inventory tracking, customer/supplier management, sales/purchase transactions, and receipt generation
- **Account management:** Authentication, account security, and password reset
- **Data synchronization:** Synchronizing your data with the server
- **Subscription management:** Premium plan verification, receipt quota tracking
- **Receipt generation:** Creating PDF and thermal printer receipts using your transaction data and company information
- **Service improvement:** Enhancing app performance and user experience

**We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.**

---

## 4. Data Security

Your data security is our priority:

### 4.1 Technical Security Measures
- **Communication encryption:** All data communication is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure authentication:** Supabase Auth infrastructure is used (JWT-based)
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** Each user can only access their own data; access to other users' data is blocked at the database level
- **Password security:** Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage
- **Subscription security:** Google Play purchases are verified with account ownership validation (obfuscated account ID)

### 4.2 Access Control
- Direct database access is disabled
- All data operations are performed through secure RPC functions
- Every request requires user authentication (JWT)
- Logo uploads are stored in user-specific folders with secure storage policies

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not share** your personal data with third parties except in the following cases:

### 5.1 Service Providers
- **Supabase:** Database, authentication, file storage (logo), and server-side functions
- **Google Play:** App distribution, updates, and subscription payment processing
- **Google ML Kit:** Barcode scanning (all processing occurs on-device, no data is sent to Google servers)
- **Google Sign-In:** Optional Google sign-in authentication (via Google Identity Services)

### 5.2 Receipt Sharing
When you share transaction receipts in PDF format, this sharing occurs through your device's share functionality. The recipients and platforms you share with are your choice.

### 5.3 Legal Requirements
Your information may be disclosed to relevant authorities in case of legal obligation or court order.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Active accounts:** Your data is retained as long as your account is active
- **Account deletion:** You can delete your account from within the app. When your account is deleted, all your business data (products, customers, suppliers, transactions), company information, receipt settings, subscription records, and company logo (if any) are permanently deleted.
- **Individual record deletion:** You can delete product, customer, supplier, and transaction records individually; deleted records cannot be recovered
- **Local data:** Upon account deletion or logout, cached data on your device is cleared; device-specific preferences (theme, language, printer settings) are preserved
